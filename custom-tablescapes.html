The idea

A table that feels like your event

Every custom tablescape is designed around your date, your venue and the mood you want guests to walk into. We set it with pieces from the Augustine rental collection, so each detail feels chosen.

We styled two looks this season to show how far a single table can travel. Fall Harvest is deep and candlelit, with white pumpkins, sage toile and burnt orange tapers. Winter is soft and glowing, with cream linen, rosemary and brass candlelight.

Pieces we like to design with

  • Toile and cream linens
  • Napkins and napkin rings
  • Brass candlesticks and candelabras
  • Crystal goblets and flatware
  • Plates and scalloped bowls
  • Taper candles in every height
  • Greenery and seasonal touches
