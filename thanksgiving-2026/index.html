Good to know

Before you pack

Bring a jacket

The cave is about 54°F all year, and the evenings by the fire pit get cold.

Times are Central

The cabin is on Central time, the same as Nashville. Check in from 4:00 PM Wednesday, check out before 10:00 AM Sunday.

Fishing

Anyone 16 or older needs a Kentucky fishing license (fw.ky.gov). Tennessee residents buy the nonresident one.

Also bring

Your own drinks, a camp chair and any snacks you can't live without.
